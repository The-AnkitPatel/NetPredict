---
title: Anomaly vs Prediction
description: Current abnormality and future congestion risk answer different questions.
---

| | Anomaly detection | Forecasting |
|---|---|---|
| Question | Is the current sample unusual? | What is the risk at a future timestamp? |
| Model | Isolation Forest (`isotree`) | Calibrated probability forest (`ranger`) |
| Inputs | Current telemetry | Current telemetry and backward-looking trends |
| Output | Anomaly score | 5, 15 and 30 minute failure probabilities |

An unusual sample does not necessarily imply an upcoming failure. A rising trend may increase future risk before the current sample becomes an anomaly.

The models are fitted on the training fold only. Calibration and test observations are not used to fit the anomaly detector.
