## Features and targets

The pipeline computes 29 features: raw metrics, 5-minute and 15-minute summaries, lagged slopes, exponentially weighted means and congestion interactions. Targets describe conditions at exactly 5, 15 or 30 minutes ahead. Unobserved future targets remain missing.

## Chronological split

The trace is split at 65% and 80%. A 30-minute gap before each boundary prevents training or calibration labels from reaching into the next fold. The final 30 observations are excluded from evaluation.

## Forecasting

Each horizon has a `ranger` probability forest plus separate RTT and packet-loss regressors. Isotonic regression calibrates probabilities on the calibration fold. The model uses 120 trees and seed 42 by default.

RTT intervals use absolute residual quantiles from the calibration fold, targeting 90% marginal coverage. Time dependence and drift can reduce observed coverage; report the measured holdout result.

## Evaluation

Compare precision, recall, F1, average precision (PR-AUC), ROC-AUC and Brier score with logistic regression, a moving-average threshold and persistence. Reliability bins show predicted probability against observed failure frequency. PSI compares feature distributions between training and test folds.

Run `Rscript scripts/train.R` to save models and the model-health report.
